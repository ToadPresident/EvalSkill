# Judgment basis and use consistency

These are implementation conventions I04 and I11. M2M supplies the qualitative labels, claim dependence, and consequential perspective, not the decision procedure below.

## Explain the selected label

For a dimension, identify (1) the inference required by this exact claim, (2) the strongest relevant support, (3) the unresolved condition or contrary evidence and why it matters, and (4) the decisive reason for the label. Explain the nearest plausible alternative briefly when it is a real choice. There is no need to recite all three definitions fifteen times.

| Label from M2M | Our working interpretation | Decisive distinction to explain |
|---|---|---|
| Reasonable | Relevant strengths adequately support the interpretation in its stated scope; weaknesses can remain. | Why the remaining weakness does not materially undermine this inference/use. |
| Proceed with caution | Substantive support exists, while unresolved conditions materially restrict how the interpretation/use can be relied upon. | What useful support survives and what prevents an unqualified judgment. |
| Insufficient | Adequate support for a necessary inference is absent, or substantial contrary evidence undermines it. | Why the gap is essential to this claim, and why existing positive evidence does not adequately supply that link. Say whether it is untested or contradicted. |

These are revisable qualitative judgments, not calibrated thresholds. An unperformed ideal study does not automatically make a judgment insufficient; examine the available direct and indirect support and its warrant. Conversely, relevant or encouraging evidence need not establish the decisive target relationship. Do not choose a harsher label merely to sound careful.

Operational satisfaction can be labelled **Reasonable*** with its exact condition; the asterisk is ours. It is not empirical proof of a broader construct. An access-pending status lies outside the rating scale. Do not add ratings into a total.

For disagreement with a published case, first align claim, use, versions, and evidence scope. Then state whether the difference comes from corrected facts, different interpretation/weighting, or changed scope. Preserve the published label. Where several labels are defensible, identify the judgment call; do not present the implementation convention as an M2M mandate.

## Fix the use before assessing consequences

Record a short use profile with an ID:

**Who interprets what result -> which decision or behavior -> whose outcome, in which setting.**

Give its owner and source. If the case source leaves a link unspecified, retain that gap or label an analyst assumption instead of inventing attribution. If several uses are relevant, keep named branches or distinct IDs and distinguish judgments where their evidence differs.

For a reproduced case, the case author's use is the comparison target. A benchmark author's original research purpose may differ and should remain separately attributed. The meaning of the selected use must persist through evidence, warrant, rating, conclusion, and revision condition. A safer narrower use can be recommended separately but cannot silently replace the assessed use.

Distinguish these questions:

- What effects have actually been measured for this use?
- What consequences can reasonably be anticipated, by which mechanism and under which assumptions?
- What finding would change the assessment of this same use?

Lack of a deployment study prevents a claim of demonstrated impact; it does not automatically prevent a justified anticipatory judgment. State a concrete supported mechanism rather than listing generic benefits and harms. A hypothetical risk alone does not determine the label. Likewise, showing that a research study is worth conducting does not establish the benefit of the application that study would investigate.

Finish with a consistency check: the use ID must retain the same interpretation, action, affected setting, and outcome throughout the argument. The ID is a checkable reference, not evidence that these elements actually stayed aligned.
