# Complete M2M report template

This is our delivery design, not M2M's prescribed report format. Use it for a complete analysis, adapting the number of claims and depth to the evidence. Default to English; honor explicit Chinese/bilingual requests. Replace authoring fields with analysis.

For a three-claim case, aim for about **3,500-5,000 English words including the evidence index**, expanding when needed to preserve a material argument. This is a writing target, not a cutoff or quality measure. Keep all five dimensions for every claim and retain the evidence, warrant, gap/satisfaction condition, rating reason, and revision condition. Save space by removing duplication, not by dropping analysis or moving decisive reasoning to a sidecar.

## Claim analyses

Repeat for every materially different claim. Start with one short paragraph explaining its route and the rule's factual application condition. Refer to the claim inventory instead of copying it. Refer to shared evidence instead of restating the entire study.

### C1 [Short title]

#### Content validity — [judgment]

[One or two paragraphs: evidence IDs and actual coverage; why it represents this claim's requirements; the material gap or satisfaction condition; why strengths and limits justify this label rather than its plausible neighbor. Expert involvement or difficulty alone is not the coverage argument.]

**Would change if:** [A concrete relevant finding and its effect, or a precise reference to a priority study below.]

#### Criterion validity — [judgment]

[Evidence of operational identity or of the required concurrent/predictive target relationship; warrant and its condition; gap or satisfaction condition; decisive rating reason. A same-item human comparator is not automatically evidence about a different target. Cite M04/M05 only where it explains a consequential choice.]

**Would change if:** [Specific relationship result and effect.]

#### Construct validity — [judgment and route condition]

[If no additional construct is required: a concise operational reason, scope, and change of claim that would reopen the question. This satisfies the section.]

[Otherwise: define the construct and necessary relationships; identify empirical support and alternatives; explain what the evidence distinguishes, what remains unresolved, and why that balance warrants the rating. A compact relationship table is useful only when it clarifies the argument. A conditional mediation analysis must not be reported as a mandatory hurdle for a direct-criterion route.]

**Would change if:** [A discriminating finding and effect, or the scope condition in the short branch.]

#### External validity — [judgment]

[Actual source and destination settings; tested transfer or relevant indirect evidence; assumption and its support; consequential missing condition or satisfaction condition; decisive reason for the rating. Distinguish same-format generalization, cross-format/domain transfer, and fixed-observation statements.]

**Would change if:** [Destination test and effect.]

#### Consequential validity — [judgment; use ID]

[Apply the recorded use without changing its meaning. Trace interpretation -> decision/behavior -> outcome. Identify measured effects and anticipated effects separately; name the evidence/assumptions behind anticipation. Explain the benefit/risk balance, remaining uncertainty, and why this label fits this use. If the record has multiple material branches, address each or state their different judgments.]

**Would change if:** [Evidence on the same use, people, and outcome, not a newly substituted research purpose.]

**Claim conclusion:** [Two or three sentences giving a usable statement, supported scope and decisive remaining dependency. Label a narrower reformulation as such. Do not recite the five sections.]

A changed use is not a directly comparable rating. Do not duplicate the full argument here.
